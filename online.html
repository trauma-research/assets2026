At the table

Common Ground online · Cards are only shown to the player who holds them, until the table reveals.

How to play

1. Choose privately. Each player sees only their own hand on their own device. They lock in one complication, or pass.

2. Reveal together. When everyone has decided, reveal the cards. Read each one and discuss who it could harm and how.

3. Vote. Each player with a card on the table votes for the one that does the most damage. Nobody can vote for their own card. If the vote is tied, you choose.

4. Agree a mitigation. Write one practical change and file it under the principle or principles it serves.

Testimony rounds. Every fourth round, each player writes one reflection on their own device. You file them under principles when the round is finished.

Running the table. Skip anyone who is away. Swap the dilemma during lock-in if it does not suit the group. When the session is over, close the game to save the library and session notes to the folder.

Anyone may pass. Do not ask people to share personal trauma; respect confidentiality; avoid graphic detail; and pause if the discussion becomes overwhelming.
