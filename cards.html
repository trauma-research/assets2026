Common Ground

A game about the ways trauma-informed research goes wrong, and how to put it right.

How to play

About five minutes a round. A dilemma is dealt and everyone plays one complication. Reveal them together, vote for the worst, then agree a mitigation. After every third round, the table reads a testimony card instead.

Nobody is asked to talk about themselves. You play a card, not an experience. Every harm on the table came out of the deck, so nothing in this game requires you to have been there. Anyone can pass at any point, for any reason or none.
