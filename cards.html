Deal a hand

Deal one scenario and one complication, and read both aloud. Everyone then writes, silently, for two minutes: what would you actually do, and what goes wrong here, and for whom. Reveal all cards at the same moment, so nobody sets the frame, then spend three minutes on where the answers differed. Either card can be swapped on its own using the button in its corner, if a hand lands somewhere the room has already been.
