Make It Worse

A card game for two to six people, about the ways research goes wrong. The Dealer turns over a scenario; everyone plays a complication to make it worse. Reveal together, agree which does the most damage, then say what would have stopped it — and which principle that answer serves. Roughly five minutes a round. After every third round the table reads a testimony card instead — one person's own account, in their own words. Nobody plays a complication on those.

Nobody is asked to talk about themselves. You play a card, not an experience. Every harm on the table came out of the deck, so nothing in this game requires you to have been there. Anyone can pass at any point, for any reason or none.

Who is playing?

Two to six. With two players you each play two complications a round.

Optional. Leave this blank and everyone gets a number.

    The good practice library

    Every fix the table agreed, filed under the principle it serves. Nothing here is scored or attributed to one person — the fixes were made together. The empty columns are as informative as the full ones: they show what this group did not reach for.

    The whole deck

    Twenty-two scenarios, four testimony cards, six complications for each scenario (plus any you add), seven principles and six personas. The printed version is A5, double-sided, and prints on an office duplex printer without trimming: download the print-ready deck (PDF).

    Add your own complication

    Write one complication for a specific dilemma. It joins that dilemma's cards on this device and is dealt whenever that dilemma is played. You can remove cards you added, but not the printed ones.

    One sentence that would make this dilemma worse.

    Type each rule up as the pairs finish it, so the sheets are already transcribed when the workshop ends. Every rule has to answer a specific harm card from Activity 1. The printed version is A3: download the rule sheet (PDF) and print one per rule, plus a parked sheet.
