How to play

1. Choose privately. Show each player their hand in turn. They can lock in a complication or pass.

2. Reveal together. Read each card and discuss who it could harm and how.

3. Vote. Choose the complication that does the most damage. You cannot vote for your own card.

4. Agree a mitigation. Write one practical change and file it under the principle or principles it serves.

Testimony rounds. Every fourth round, while testimony cards remain, reflect on the account and record what should have happened.
