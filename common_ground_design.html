How to play

1. Choose privately. Show each player their hand in turn. They can lock in a constraint or pass.

2. Reveal together. Turn the cards over at the same moment, so nobody sets the frame.

3. Draw a person. The Dealer turns over a people card and reads it aloud. Nobody at this table chose who it would be. One sentence each: what does this design, under these constraints, now do to them?

4. Vote. Choose the constraint that does the most damage. You cannot vote for your own card. That point is the only thing scored.

5. Find common ground. Agree one sentence on what would have prevented it, file it under the principle or principles it serves, and name the signal it acts on.

You can pass at any time. Do not ask anyone to disclose personal experiences. Game data is stored locally in this browser on this device.
